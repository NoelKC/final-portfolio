H E L L O

Gia Noel Curtis is a recent graduate of San Jose State University . Receiving a Bachelors in Digital Media Art with honors (Summa Cum Laude).Digital Media Art allowed her to explore the wide field of digital mediums. She is primarily a digital media artist but likes to dabble into multimedia.


She does like (front end) web design and has made sites using HTML, CSS, Javascript, and Three.JS. She is familiar with Adobe software like Premiere Pro, Photoshop and Illustrator. She has some experience with laser cutting, making 3D models, and 3D printing.


She is always up to learning new things and doing the hard work.
