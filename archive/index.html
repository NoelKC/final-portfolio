Becoming a Better Gardener


May 16, 2025

People who know me know that I love plants. I have many plants and take care of them everyday. So I made this device to help me become a better gardener. The handle has lights that help me perfect the angle of holding the watering can while the soil sensor rates the moisture of the soil to help me know how much water to give. The main materials to use this were an Adafruit Feather, IMU, Neopixel, 8X8 LED Display, and a soil sensor all coded using Arduino.

MMIP Awareness


Dec 12, 2022

My mom and I are Indigenous, my mom’s grandfather would tell her that we weren't Mexican, we were just American. He never shared much about his background, heritage, or language with his children. When he passed, we then learned how much of our blood is Indigenous, with my grandma being around 70%. When we found out we didn’t change our lives to adopt Indigenous culture but we did become more aware of their struggles, like the Murdered Indigenous Persons (MMIP) crisis . There was a study in 2016 that found that 84.3 percent of American Indian and Alaska Native women have experienced violence in their lifetime, including 56.1 percent who have experienced sexual violence (Indian Affairs). Indigenous people are still suffering and their issues are being ignored. This is why this project is crucial since it brings more awareness.

Trapped Creativity


May 11, 2024

My inspiration for this work is the overwhelming creative block that I have been building up. I feel that I have been doing art so much more for school than for myself. Even in these photos you can see the computer has Canvas open. The majority of my creativity has been dedicated to my school projects that I have little to spare in my free time. Ignoring past projects and drawing that I have left incomplete for years. The ghost drawing shown in these photos is one that I have drawn since I was a freshman in high school. Something I constantly draw to represent myself, almost like an artist persona. I used to make comics with it all the time but now I have been putting all my notebooks in storage to make room for school projects. In the first photos you can see I am storing them next to my trash can. So in this piece I wanted to show my childhood creativity trying to be free while I am dedicated to school in the background. But in the end instead of locking that side of myself up I decide to make space for it and myself. To make time for myself and my own personal projects.

broken memories

Sep 24, 2025

I am lucky to have been alive to meet my great grandma. She was in my life until I was 16 and when I was little I would often be at her house to help my grandma take care of her. But sadly I never felt that I knew my great grandma since she suffered from severe dementia. She would have a few good days but the majority of the time she had bad days where she wouldn't speak or acknowledge anyone. I always heard about her, many people had stories about how creative and kind she used to be. But I was never able to experience her myself, to truly meet her. So this piece is a representation of how I felt dementia took my great grandma away from me.

Crochet

Oct 15, 2023

Crochet is known to be a craft the is really strenuous on the hands and wrists. So in this video I wanted to show all of the work that goes into crocheting a piece (even something small). The counter in the corner shows the total number of stitches I made (counting increases as 2 stitches and decreases as 1). In the raw footage I was counting the number of stitches but the video was too long (and I messed up the count a couple of times) so I used the counter to get the same message across. In the background I am playing audio I recorded of my wrists and knuckles popping. My wrists are pretty bad sounding and the majority of the sound is just from me rotating my wrists in circles. It honestly surprised me how bad my own wrists sounded when isolated. It goes to show how this craft can wear you down. But even so I will keep on crocheting over and over again.

Powerful Shadows

Nov 24, 2025

I was inspired by the "What's that Pokemon?" segment from the anime and I wanted to do my own take on it. So I recorded the shadows of the figures I have collected. I collect a lot of different types of figures from more detailed to more cheesy, but they all mean a lot to me. The stories they are connected to inspire me and my own work and I wanted to show my appreciation of them and show how they have impacted others as well. I bet many people also feel connected to these characters and can also recognize their shadows. Showing how iconic they are and how they have touched so many people. I put the shadows in order of who I believe is most to least recognizable and I edited sound clips that connect to each character and what I remember them by.

Manufactured Femininity


Dec 6, 2023

Corsets in the past made it so women can modify themselves into this manufactured mold of what femininity was at the time. They enlarge the butt and breast by making their waist so thin you can wrap your fingers around it. Today these standards pushed onto female bodies are slowly eroding. People are breaking out of these molds and embracing who they are. So in my piece, Manufactured Femininity, I wanted to show this erosion of past molds. I was heavily inspired by old corset ad photos showing women with super thin waists. Specifically, I found the Warner’s “Rust-Proof” corset . I thought it was ironic that I am making a rusty corset based off of their “Rust-Proof” one. So I used it as a guide while I made my piece.

Chachayotes

Apr 19, 2024

I made Chachayotes out of ceramic and I used steel wire and leather to attach the pieces together. Chachayotes are traditionally made out of leather with ayoyote seed shells sewed on to them. They are worn by Aztec dancers who will wear these on their legs, arms, and have rattles with similar seed shells. This means a lot to me since my mom is an Aztec dancer. I am Native American but I didn’t grow up with a lot of my culture. So the few things I do have I cherish. Especially since there is an extreme amount of Native American cultural erasure that goes back decades.

{Pixel Self}


Apr, 2025

I made this pixel art of myself melting a year earlier and I wanted to see how I could translate it into print. First, I made a simplified version of my original pixel art. I then made a 32 x 32 grid on a piece of linoleum in Sharpie. This was a reduction print so every time I moved to the next color I looked at a key and removed the squares on the grid. I kept a record of every layer and all of my experiments.
