H E L L O

I am Gia Noel Curtis, or GNKC on most platforms. I am a recent graduate from San Jose State University (SJSU), Bachelors of Fine Art in Digital Media Art. I graduated with honors with a GPA of 3.875 (Summa Cum Laude / Dean’s Scholar) in 2025.


Before SJSU I did a year at a trait school called Silicon Valley Career Technical Education (SVCTE) for Animation. I got in through my high school, spending half the day there and taking the bus to SVCTE for three hour afternoon classes to learn all I could. I did learn the basics and got a certificate of completion. Although I like Animation the process is very draining so that is why I chose a different major when applying to SJSU.


Out of all of the art majors Digital Media Art is the one that stood out to me since of how wide the field is. You get to learn a multitude of different digital mediums. Which appealed to me since I love to learn new things. Right now I am job hunting. The job market is rough right now but I am not going to let it bring me down.
